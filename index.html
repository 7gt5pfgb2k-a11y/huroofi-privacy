Privacy Policy

Huroofi is a learning app for children aged 3 to 6 and their parents.

We do not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no third parties tracking use.

  • Lesson progress, stars and the chosen language are stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Speech comes from the device's built-in text-to-speech.
  • The app does not use the microphone or the camera, and finger tracing is never saved or sent.
  • The app contains no links to other websites and no way to contact other users.

Contact: razanqadada1@gmail.com

If this policy changes, we will update it here.
